## What changes vs. what compounds

Set your expectations correctly and the firehose loses its power to overwhelm:

**Will change, repeatedly:** model names and capabilities, tool features and syntax, config formats, which products are hot, pricing. Everything in this course that lives behind a "check the current docs" note (hooks syntax, MCP configs, model tiers) was written that way *because* it changes.

**Will compound, quietly:** everything else you learned. The loop (4). Context engineering (5). Briefing and verification (2.3, 4.4). Trust boundaries (8.5). Orchestration judgment (10.5). Your harness, your library, your receipts. When the next big capability drops — and it will, repeatedly — notice what actually happens to you now: *your harness absorbs it.* New model? Change one config line; your CLAUDE.md, Skills, hooks, and contracts all still apply. Better agents? Your orchestration skill just got more leverage. The people who must start over each wave are the ones who learned buttons. You learned the machine.

## The one-hour week

A staying-current system, sized to reality:

- **30 min — release notes over hot takes.** Claude Code's changelog and Anthropic's announcements: what actually shipped. One secondary source you trust for the wider field (Karpathy's occasional videos remain the gold standard for signal; the [Anthropic Academy](https://www.anthropic.com/learn/build-with-claude) courses when something big lands). Skip: launch-day threads, doom/hype cycles, anything with "🤯" in the title.
- **20 min — try ONE thing.** New feature, new server, new pattern — in a throwaway project, with the 2.5 four-question filter. One real trial beats fifty read-abouts, and most things fail the filter, which is the filter working. What passes: ratchet it into the harness (a CLAUDE.md line, a Skill update, a policy change) — that's how "staying current" becomes *compounding* instead of churn.
- **10 min — teach it forward.** One message: the thing you tried, told to one person who'd benefit. Teaching is the best retention technology ever found, and it builds the audience 11.4 said doors two and three want. Also it's just the right thing to do — more on that in a second.

That's the whole system. Anyone telling you staying current requires drowning in feeds is selling the feed.
