## Video timestamp review

The Chapter 12 guide links directly to the published video chapter markers. Before
recording derivative lessons:

1. Open the timestamp and confirm the segment still begins where the lesson says it does.
2. Record the video title, channel, access date, and any meaningful edit or chapter change.
3. Pair the segment with current primary documentation.
4. Mark the lesson as video-reported, provider-confirmed, or both.
5. Update the lab if the product surface or exam claim has changed.

## Claim policy

- Never describe this chapter or the companion video as the certification itself.
- Never promise a job, client, revenue, salary, or exam pass.
- Never publish a current eligibility, price, score, or renewal claim without a dated source review.
- Keep provider-specific syntax in the engineer lane tied to the current official reference.
- Preserve simulations for learners who lack account access; label them as simulations.
- Record corrections in the pull request or maintenance review that changes the lesson.
