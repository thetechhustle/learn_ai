# Assessment Guide

These assessments measure whether you can make sound decisions with an AI agent, not whether you can remember product trivia. Use them to establish a baseline, practice at each chapter boundary, and document growth.

## Assessment path

1. Before Chapter 1, complete the entry form in [Paired Diagnostics](diagnostic.md#entry-diagnostic).
2. After each chapter, complete its task in [Chapter Checkpoints](chapter-checkpoints.md).
3. Use the [Answer Key and Evidence Guide](answer-key.md) to self-score. When possible, ask a peer to score from the same evidence without coaching.
4. After Chapter 11, complete the exit form in [Paired Diagnostics](diagnostic.md#exit-diagnostic).
5. Compare entry and exit results with the [Competency Framework](competency-framework.md), then choose your next practice target.

The chapter tasks complement the [Capstone](../capstone.md). They are smaller competency checks; the capstone asks you to combine the competencies in one responsible build.

## Fair-use conditions

- Use the lane that matches your current work. A no-code artifact and an engineer artifact can earn the same score when they provide equivalent evidence.
- Submit in writing, as an audio or video explanation, or through a live demonstration. Include links or timestamps so a reviewer can locate the evidence.
- Use screen-reader, captioning, speech-to-text, keyboard, display, or translation support as needed. The assessment does not reward typing speed or polished English.
- Work untimed unless a real constraint is part of the task. Take breaks.
- Use synthetic or public data. Never expose secrets, regulated data, client-confidential material, or personal data to complete an assessment.
- You may use an AI assistant except where a prompt asks for your independent judgment. Disclose what it contributed and verify the result yourself.
- If a service, account, payment method, or local installation is unavailable, use the documented simulation option. Score the quality of the decision and evidence, not tool access.

## Keep an evidence folder

Create one folder in your course journal:

```text
assessment/
  entry/
  chapter-01/
  chapter-02/
  ...
  chapter-11/
  exit/
```

Each folder should contain or link to the artifact, your score, the evidence for that score, feedback received, and one next action. Do not publish the folder unless every item is safe to share.
